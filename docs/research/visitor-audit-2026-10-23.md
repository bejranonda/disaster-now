# Visitor audit: ภัยรอบบ้าน ตอนนี้ / Disaster Now (23 Oct 2026)

> An independent visitor audit. I did not build the page; I read it as its real readers would. No file in the repository was changed except this report.

## How it was done

- **When:** 23 Oct 2026, 20:28–21:02 UTC (24 Oct, 03:28–04:02 Bangkok, a Saturday). That is night in Thailand, so UV is "night" and "today" means the rest of Saturday.
- **Where:** headless Chromium 141 (`/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`) through the session's egress proxy, on a cloud host outside Thailand. The page was served by `node tools/serve.mjs`; for the offline test it was served by a read-only HTTPS copy in the scratchpad, because the service worker registers only on `https:`.
- **The code moved during the audit.**
  - It started on commit `19fc94c`. `f717e8a` landed at 20:40, and from about 20:55 the working tree held uncommitted changes: a heat "calibration" (`calibration.js`), `actions.js`, `levels.js` and others.
  - Every finding below was re-checked on the state served between 20:57 and 21:00 UTC. Findings that depend on the uncommitted work are marked **(WIP)**.
- **Live sources:**
  - Open-Meteo (ensemble, air quality, elevation), GISTDA, ThaiWater, HII, EMSC, USGS, GDACS, JMA and DMR were called live.
  - The collector's files in `public/data/live/` (TMD warnings, TMD quakes, TMD storms, FIRMS) were used as they stood. They were refreshed at about 20:25 and 20:48 UTC.
- **Air4Thai:** the mirror `air4thai.com` was refused by this Chromium (`net::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`; curl: "unable to get local issuer certificate", the same incomplete chain as KI-002). To see what a phone that fetches the missing intermediate would see, I served it from a copy fetched at 20:30 UTC with the two Let's Encrypt intermediates in `tools/certs/`. Without that copy, PM2.5 falls back to GISTDA everywhere (see V27).
- **Open-Meteo from this host was flaky:** HTTP 429 on elevation, and 12-second timeouts on air quality and the ensemble in about a third of the loads. Some live cards therefore show "ดึงข้อมูลไม่สำเร็จ". I used those as natural tests of a failing source, not as page bugs.
- **Pinned states for the UX scout's §6 script:**
  - They are built from `test/fixtures/` (Bangkok, clock fixed at 23 Oct 19:50 UTC).
  - `calm`: ensemble temperatures −7 °C. `dust3`: PCD station at 52.0 µg/m³. `warning`: the real TMD heavy-rain CAP item from the collector, extended to Bangkok and to 24 Oct 18:00.
  - `stale`: every Bangkok PCD station 5 h old, ensemble down. `staleNoGistda`: the same, with GISTDA also down. `wet`: rain ×20 and gusts +40 km/h tomorrow.
  - Scripts: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/d0c0b487-5c79-5ab8-a62d-123ead309e6f/scratchpad/` (`fx.mjs`, `script6.mjs`, `script12.mjs`, `offline.mjs`, `misc.mjs`, `persona*.mjs`, `live_places.mjs`, `noon.mjs`, `evening.mjs`, `weight.mjs`, `flicker.mjs`).
- **Places:**
  - Bangkok old town, Chiang Mai, Hat Yai, Phuket town, Ubon Ratchathani, Mae Sai and a rural point (16.5, 103.0), each by `#p=`, plus `#t=100103`, `#t=500101` and GPS.
  - The clock was moved to 12:30 and 19:30 Bangkok for two extra runs, using live forecasts. The PCD and water data were stale in those runs, so only UV, heat, rain and the table were read from them.
- **Not done:** real phones, the real LINE app, iOS Safari, GPS inside LINE, screen readers and a native Thai reviewer. LINE was emulated by user agent only.

## The short version

On an ordinary dry October night, **no place I checked was calm.**

- With the committed code, all 7 places said **เตรียมตัว** (get ready) because of tomorrow afternoon's heat index.
- With the working-tree calibration, Bangkok, Ubon and the rural point said **ทำทันที** (act now, crimson) at 03:56, about a heat index of 43 °C tomorrow afternoon with a 60 % chance.

Meanwhile, in Hat Yai and Phuket, where TMD had a **Severe heavy-rain warning in force**, the verdict's headline was heat. The rain tile was yellow, or blue when the ensemble timed out. The pre-warnings said "nothing unusual".

An older relative opening the LINE link would conclude that something must be done every day, about the wrong thing. Three engineering faults compound this:

- After one visit, the offline page is dead: `actions.js` is missing from the service worker's shell.
- A failed source is counted as calm for rain, wind and flood.
- The exact GPS fix shows on screen and goes into the FloodWatch link.

Of the 12 tasks in the UX scout's script, 1 passes (task 4), 2 nearly pass (tasks 2 and 5) and 9 fail.

## What worked well

- **No location prompt on load** (0 `getCurrentPosition` calls). Every provider request carried the point rounded to 2 decimals. A GPS share goes by subdistrict code (`#t=100107`).
- **The local subdistrict search is excellent.** It answers in 20 ms with no request while typing, separates duplicates (บางรัก ×6) with district and province, and takes an exact district name straight there on Enter.
- **Street search follows the rules:** it runs only on a tap or Enter, says beforehand that the text leaves the phone, waits ≥ 1.1 s between calls and tries a "ซอย…" variant.
- **No horizontal scroll** at 320 px with ก+. ก+ is remembered, and with it no text is under 14 px.
- **Colour is never the only signal on tiles and verdict:** each has a word and a 4-dot meter. "No data" is hatched with dashes and never shows a level word.
- **The calm fixture gives a correct calm verdict** ("ยังไม่น่าห่วง · ทุกเรื่องอยู่ในระดับต่ำสุดของหน่วยงาน ณ ตอนนี้"). `dust3` gives the right PCD word with its sourced action ("ใส่หน้ากากกันฝุ่น PM2.5 ทุกครั้งที่ออกนอกบ้าน").
- **Every card names its source and time** and marks model values. Distances to stations are given. The quake card says earthquakes can't be forecast and that soft ground shakes more.
- **Probabilities are words with rounded chances** ("ไม่ถึง 10%", "เกิน 90%"). There are no countdowns and no clock times in the outlook.
- **Missing sources never crashed the page:** no page errors in any run, including all sources down. Fewer than half the tiles answering gives "ยังไม่มีข้อมูลพอ" (KI-F02).
- **Points outside Thailand get a clear message.** `#t=` links work, e.g. `#t=500101` → ต.ศรีภูมิ อ.เมืองเชียงใหม่.
- **Dark mode is legible** (7.7–15.8:1) apart from one badge (V23).
- **The English is complete and mostly natural British English.** The LINE share uses LINE's documented URL scheme.
